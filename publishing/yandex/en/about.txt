One clever move can change the whole board. In Pair Up, slide matching pieces together and clear the targets to solve each puzzle.

Explore colorful worlds, from desert temples and frozen portals to volcanoes and islands in the sky. Use pipes, teleporters, keys, locks and bombs, and watch how gravity changes your next move.

Earn bonus points for spare moves and time. Replay levels to improve your personal best, try a different approach with Undo, or restart when you need a fresh look. Play in portrait or landscape, with optional zoom controls for smaller screens.
