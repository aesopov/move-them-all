Clear all target pieces. The level goals show which types to remove and how many remain.

Drag a movable piece horizontally or vertically to bring matching pieces together. Use pipes and teleporters, match keys to locks, and plan around gravity and hazardous tiles.

On mobile, enable zoom mode to pinch and pan. Tap a piece, then tap a destination in the same row or column.

Use Undo or Restart to try another approach. Finish with spare moves and time to earn bonus points and improve your best score.
