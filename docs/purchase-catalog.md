# Purchase catalog

Names are limited to 100 characters; descriptions to 200. All 91 entries have been checked.

This is a copy catalog, not a Yandex bulk-import file. Prices are intentionally omitted. World unlocks include every level in the named world. Unlock-all includes future campaign levels. Skips are single-use, do not expire, and are spent after free skips.

All 11 world products are listed; publish only those that offer locked levels. Existing world names are taken from `localization/messages.csv`. The CSV source is `localization/purchases.csv`.
