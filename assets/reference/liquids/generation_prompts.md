# Liquid assets

Generated using built-in image_gen with assets/reference/master_style.png as style reference. Brief: prompts/04_liquids.md. Original surface/body atlases preserved here. Runtime PNGs are in assets/liquids. Preparation: `bash tools/prepare_liquid_assets.sh`. The renderer selects a surface where the cell above differs, otherwise a body, and retains animated glints/bubbles plus procedural fallback for missing PNGs.

## Preparation and verification

The preparation script applies symmetric edge blending to the generated tiles and blends each surface's bottom edge into its body. Pixel comparisons confirmed zero differing pixels at all twelve tested boundaries: surface left/right, body left/right, body top/bottom, and surface-bottom/body-top for each liquid. All outputs are 128x128. Surface PNGs retain alpha above the wave; body PNGs are opaque.

Godot 4.7.1 imported and rendered the assets successfully. `in_game.png` shows all three multi-cell pools. `preview_level.json` is an art fixture outside the campaign folders, not a designed puzzle. Generated waves are static; existing procedural glints and bubbles remain animated. The source atlases remain unchanged.
